How this scan works

The {{ report.checks_run or 23 }} checks we ran

Online foundation

  • Google Business Profile completeness
  • Google rating and review count
  • Reputation on other review sites (Yelp, Angi, BBB and others)
  • Social media presence
  • AI assistant recommendations (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini, each searching the web live)
  • Google map ranking, for your name and for what a new customer would search
  • Name and phone consistency across directories

Website

  • Mobile load speed, scored out of 10 (middle result of 3 Google speed tests)
  • Mobile-friendly layout
  • Clear call-to-action on the first screen
  • Right main action for your type of business
  • Focused page (not too many competing goals)
  • Working call-to-action links
  • Tap-to-call
  • "Text Us" option
  • Online booking or ordering
  • Live chat or AI chat for after-hours visitors
  • Contact form
  • Overall performance score (Google PageSpeed)
  • Accessibility score (Google)

Paid advertising

  • Ads currently running (Google, Meta, Yelp and others)
  • Ad tags for tracking and retargeting, including inside Google Tag Manager
  • Competitor ads in your local search

Slow lead response, after-hours coverage and dormant leads are estimated from your answers on the scan form, plus your website and Google hours.

About these numbers

Scores reflect what we found when we ran this scan on {{ scan_date }}. Search rankings, reviews, AI assistant answers and website speed change over time and can differ by location and device, so a scan on another day may show different results.

Dollar figures are estimates, not measurements. They apply general formulas based on published industry research and our own stated assumptions to the numbers you gave us on the scan form (leads, close rate, average job value and response time). They show what each gap could be costing you, rounded to whole or half jobs and shown as "up to" amounts. They are not based on your actual sales data and are not a guarantee of results or of what any fix will earn.

Some findings rely on third-party sources such as Google, review sites and AI assistants, which can be incomplete or out of date. Where we couldn't confirm something, we say so and leave it out of the totals. This report is for general informational purposes and is not financial, legal or accounting advice.
