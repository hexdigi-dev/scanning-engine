About these numbers

Scores reflect what we found when we ran this scan on {{ scan_date }}. Search rankings, reviews, AI assistant answers and website speed change over time and can differ by location and device, so a scan on another day may show different results.

Dollar figures are estimates, not measurements. They apply general formulas based on published industry research and our own stated assumptions to the numbers you gave us on the scan form (leads, close rate, average job value and response time). They show what each gap could be costing you, rounded to whole or half jobs and shown as "up to" amounts. They are not based on your actual sales data and are not a guarantee of results or of what any fix will earn.

Some findings rely on third-party sources such as Google, review sites and AI assistants, which can be incomplete or out of date. Where we couldn't confirm something, we say so and leave it out of the totals. This report is for general informational purposes and is not financial, legal or accounting advice.
